What happens at close

  1. 1. Bidding locks — no new bids, edits, or cancellations.
  2. 2. For each class, the highest bids win seats up to the seat cap.
  3. 3. Ties for the last seat are broken by a random draw.
  4. 4. Every resolved bid is deducted — losing bids are not refunded.
  5. 5. Students see their outcomes on their Results page.
